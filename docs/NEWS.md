# News

Curated highlights for the stem-lesson-quiz-game. For the full dated log, see [docs/CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## v26.07 - 2026-07-02

### Highlights

- Three game modes (Quick Run, Challenge, Endless) with a per-mode 4 / 6 / 8
  choice selector and a 140-stem question bundle.
- 15-theme cosmetic shop grouped into Starter, World, Mascot, and Ultimate
  sections, with tap-to-preview and a buy-confirmation modal.
- Daily goals grew to 5-per-day with completion bonuses (+50 coins at 3 goals,
  +150 at all 5).
- Mastery redesigned as a Wordle-style trophy screen covering all 20 lessons at
  a glance, with a Share button.
- Coins are now the single reward number; the separate score system was removed.

### Upgrade notes

- The save format advanced to schema v3. Existing v2 saves migrate
  automatically and keep coins, streaks, mastery, and themes; older v1 saves are
  reset. If GitHub Pages buttons look stuck, hard-refresh (Cmd+Shift+R) or use
  the "Reset save" button on the Mastery screen.
